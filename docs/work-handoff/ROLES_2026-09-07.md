# Roles — owner decision 2026-09-07

- Owner is the property business owner. Existing owner password is unchanged.
- Admin is the operational manager: member invitations, role changes, suspension of non-admin staff, recovery, shared recovery settings, mail configuration. Only Owner/System God can suspend or demote Admin.
- Admin may only grant property scopes contained in their own scope and manage members whose full scope is contained in theirs. Shared mail/recovery settings remain workspace-wide.
- Owner cannot be edited by the member API. System God cannot be provisioned, suspended, demoted, reinvited or reset through normal member APIs (including by Owner).
- System God has all properties in this workspace. Provisioning uses a distinct member identity with a unique activation token; no shared owner password. Normal 30-day sessions/device history and personal password setup apply.
- Authenticated actor ID is recorded for member and recovery mutations.
- This deployment has one workspace containing Sweetfun and Offland. Cross-customer workspace switching and tenant-specific owners/storage are not yet implemented; do not claim a multi-tenant God console exists.
- New operator identity is created through the server-only provisionOperator helper; it is not an assignable member role. Future operator email transfer must preserve audit identity and invalidate old credentials/sessions.

Production rollout: existing designated operator account was active as Admin. Promoted it in place with expected-state CAS, preserving its credential and account ID; readback confirmed God/all-properties and unchanged credential. No activation email or password reset was needed. Existing owner's credential was not modified.

Phone account support: login accepts Taiwan mobile numbers in local or +886 format, normalized to the same identity and rate-limit bucket. Explicitly provisioned phone-only members may use administrator-supplied personal passwords without email activation or forced reset. Passwords remain scrypt-hashed. Duplicate phone identities are rejected; ambiguous login matches fail closed. New phone-only provisioning is currently a server-side operation, not the email invitation form.

Login-only access (owner decision): anonymous calendar access is removed. Main workspace layout verifies the server session before rendering and redirects to login; client guard also hides content on missing/expired membership. Calendar API denies missing/invalid sessions before any source reads, retains forced-reset protection, and never falls back to anonymous operational data. Login, activation and password-help pages remain reachable.
